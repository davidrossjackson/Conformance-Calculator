What is conformance?

Conformance means being where you are supposed to be, doing what you are scheduled to do, when you are scheduled to do it.

In a contact center or workforce management context:

  • Taking calls when scheduled
  • Taking breaks when scheduled
  • Attending scheduled meetings
  • Taking lunch at the scheduled time
